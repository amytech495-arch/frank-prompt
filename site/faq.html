Frequently Asked Questions

Is Frank Prompt free?

Yes. All 361 prompts are free for registered users. A Pro plan with premium features is coming soon.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You need a free account to browse and view prompts. Sign up with email or Google in seconds.

How do I use a prompt?

Open any prompt, click "Copy prompt", then paste it into your AI video tool (e.g. Runway, Pika, Sora, Veo).

Can I download prompts?

Yes. Every prompt page has a Download button to save the prompt as a text file.

How do I contact support?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.
