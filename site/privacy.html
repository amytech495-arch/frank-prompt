Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. What we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your name and profile information from Google. We use Firebase Authentication (Google) to manage accounts securely.

Usage data. We store which prompts you save or interact with, tied to your account, so your library works across devices. This is stored in Google Firestore.

Preferences. Your language choice and theme (dark/light mode) are stored locally in your browser via localStorage. This never leaves your device.

2. How we store it

Your account and prompt data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore database) with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by security rules so only you can read your own data — other users cannot see your account or saved prompts.

3. How we use it

We use your data solely to operate Frank Prompt: authenticating you, showing your saved prompts, and improving the service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-party services

We use the following services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database
  • Google Sign-In — optional login method
  • Vercel — website hosting

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your language, theme, and login session. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp. Deleting your account removes your profile and saved prompts from our database.

7. Children's privacy

Frank Prompt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be noted here with a revised date.
