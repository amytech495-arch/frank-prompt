MASTER PROMPT — INDOOR FIRE RING {CREATURE} OBSTACLE CHALLENGE

30 Seconds | 9:16 Portrait | 4K Cinematic Realism | Ultra-Realistic Live Action

VIDEO PROMPT

Create a 30-second ultra-realistic 4K cinematic live-action obstacle performance video in vertical 9:16 portrait format.

The entire video takes place inside a massive indoor arena hall similar to a professional circus, elite stunt competition, or world-class live performance event. The arena is filled with thousands of spectators, dramatic overhead spotlights, dark atmospheric background, stage smoke, and a high-energy performance atmosphere.

The challenge is a professional extreme obstacle performance, combining balance, acrobatics, fire ring jumping, featured {CREATURE} performance, and spring-launch stunts.

CHARACTER LOCK (VERY IMPORTANT)

The main performer is a 20-year-old Tier-1-country female adaptive athlete and acrobat.

Appearance:

{HAIR_DESCRIPTION}
athletic slim body
strong legs
confident facial expression
red professional acrobat performance outfit
white athletic shoes

IMPORTANT: BOTH OF HER HANDS ARE AMPUTATED.

Her arms end naturally above the wrists.
She has no hands visible anywhere in the video.

She completes the entire performance using:

leg strength
balance
body control
athletic movement

Maintain the same:

face
hairstyle
red outfit
body shape
amputated hands
character identity

throughout the entire 30 seconds.

ARENA SETUP

Inside the indoor hall, create:

large audience surrounding the arena
bright professional stage lighting
elevated metal starting platform
several wooden stools
one chair
one long bench obstacle
giant circular flaming ring suspended vertically
decorated / performance-ready {CREATURE} standing in the middle of the stage
large round spring-loaded bounce platform positioned after the creature
final elevated landing stool/platform

The layout must be clearly visible.

The obstacle path order:

HIGH PLATFORM → STOOLS → CHAIR → BENCH → FIRE RING → {CREATURE} BACK / BODY PLATFORM → ROUND SPRING PLATFORM → FIRE RING AGAIN → FINAL HIGH STOOL

TIMELINE
0–4 SECONDS — STARTING PLATFORM

Wide cinematic establishing shot.

The adaptive female athlete stands on top of a tall metal platform.

The audience watches with anticipation.

The camera slowly pushes toward her.

Show clearly:

her amputated arms
her determined face
the flaming ring in the distance
the featured {CREATURE} in the arena

The crowd reacts with anticipation.

4–9 SECONDS — OBSTACLE RUN

She jumps down from the high platform.

She moves through the obstacle sequence:

jumps onto first stool
jumps across another stool
lands on the chair
crosses the long bench

Her movement is controlled and athletic.

Show:

precise foot placement
balance
powerful jumps
audience reactions

Use slow-motion moments.

9–14 SECONDS — FIRST FIRE RING JUMP

She accelerates toward the flaming ring.

IMPORTANT:

The flaming ring is a complete circular hoop.

She must jump DIRECTLY THROUGH THE CENTER OPENING OF THE FIRE RING.

The camera must clearly show:

the full circle of fire
the empty center hole
her entire body traveling through the middle

She does NOT jump beside the ring.
She does NOT jump over the ring.
She passes through the exact center.

Use dramatic slow motion:

flames surrounding the outside edge
hair moving
crowd screaming

After passing through the fire ring, she lands safely on the {CREATURE}.

14–19 SECONDS — {CREATURE} PERFORMANCE

The featured {CREATURE} moves around the arena stage in a dramatic, controlled performance moment.

The athlete stands balanced on the creature’s back / body platform area.

The crowd cheers loudly.

Show:

the {CREATURE} moving around the stage
audience excitement
spotlight following her
her maintaining balance without hands

Make it feel like a world-class performance.

If the creature is fantasy-based such as dragon or dinosaur, keep it visually photorealistic and believable inside the arena performance environment.

19–24 SECONDS — {CREATURE} TO SPRING PLATFORM

The {CREATURE} approaches the final stunt area.

A large round spring-loaded bounce platform is directly beside it.

She jumps from the creature’s back / body platform.

She lands in the CENTER of the round bounce platform.

The platform compresses downward realistically.

There is a brief moment of tension.

Then the spring mechanism launches her upward into the air.

24–28 SECONDS — FINAL FIRE RING PASS

The spring launches her high above the arena.

She travels upward toward the flaming ring.

IMPORTANT:

She must pass through the EXACT CENTER OF THE FIRE RING AGAIN.

Show clearly:

her body entering the middle opening
the fire ring surrounding her
her body completely crossing through the circle

The camera captures:

front view
side view
slow-motion aerial shot

This is the biggest moment of the performance.

28–30 SECONDS — FINAL LANDING

After passing through the fire ring, she lands perfectly on the elevated final stool/platform.

She stands in a victory pose.

The crowd explodes with applause.

The {CREATURE} remains visible in the background.

The flaming ring glows behind her.

End with a cinematic hero shot.

CAMERA STYLE

Ultra-realistic cinematic filming:

4K resolution
vertical 9:16
professional sports camera
dramatic slow motion
close-up facial shots
low-angle hero shots
wide arena shots
tracking camera movement
realistic fire lighting
realistic crowd reactions
AUDIO DESIGN

Raw synchronized sounds:

crowd cheering
audience gasps
footsteps on wood and metal
{CREATURE} movement sounds
fire crackling
fire whoosh during jumps
spring platform impact sound
final applause
NEGATIVE PROMPT

Avoid:

normal hands appearing
extra arms
changing character appearance
outdoor scenes
ocean/water
jumping beside the fire ring
missing the center of the ring
passing around the ring
cartoon style
fake CGI look
unrealistic physics
duplicated characters
distorted body anatomy

30 seconds
9:16 vertical portrait
4K ultra-realistic cinematic live action
indoor arena obstacle challenge
adaptive female acrobat with both hands amputated
fire ring center jump → {CREATURE} landing → spring launch → second fire ring center jump → final victory landing
