ULTRA MASTER PROMPT — RAW WILDLIFE SURVIVAL VIDEO (VERTICAL 9:16)

Raw 15-second vertical 9:16 wildlife survival video, filmed on iPhone 17 Pro Max back camera, fixed tripod position, realistic documentary observation angle 
overlooking a dangerous riverbank. Camera completely locked for the entire video. No camera movement, no panning, no tilting, no tracking, no zooming, no drone 
shots. iPhone, tripod, operator, reflections, shadows, equipment, vehicles, humans never visible. Pure wildlife footage only.

Environment:

Harsh dry jungle wilderness mixed with desert-like terrain. Dead trees, dry grass, cracked earth, dusty air, muddy riverbank, natural sunlight, realistic heat 
haze, authentic African ecosystem. River inhabited by multiple massive crocodiles. Natural weather conditions. No staged appearance.

Style:

Ultra-realistic wildlife documentary. National Geographic quality realism. Authentic predator survival behavior. Real animal movement physics. Natural ecosystem
 interaction. Raw field-recorded footage. No cinematic effects. No Hollywood action styling. No fantasy elements. No exaggerated animal abilities.

Subject:

A large exhausted male lion suffering from thirst and fatigue. Multiple giant crocodiles dominate the river territory. The lion must approach the water 
carefully while avoiding deadly ambush predators.

[00:00 – 00:03]

Fixed camera overlooking muddy riverbank.

A lone exhausted lion slowly emerges from dry grass and sparse dead trees. Dust blows across cracked earth. The lion cautiously approaches the water, scanning 
for danger. Natural body language shows fatigue and thirst.

Audio only: dry wind, insects, distant birds, faint river sounds.

[00:03 – 00:06]

The lion reaches the river edge and lowers its head to drink.

Small ripples begin appearing nearby on the muddy water surface. Subtle movement beneath the water suggests hidden crocodiles. The lion remains unaware for a 
brief moment.

Audio only: drinking sounds, gentle water movement, insects, wind.

[00:06 – 00:09]

Without warning, a giant crocodile explodes from the river.

Massive splash of muddy water erupts into the air. The crocodile lunges toward the lion's head and neck. The lion instantly jumps backward and releases a 
powerful defensive roar.

Realistic predator ambush behavior.

Audio only: violent splash, crocodile impact, lion roar, heavy breathing.

[00:09 – 00:12]

A brutal survival struggle unfolds directly in front of the fixed camera.

The lion swipes with powerful claws while backing away. The crocodile repeatedly snaps its jaws and thrashes in shallow water. Mud, water droplets, and dust 
scatter naturally.

Realistic animal combat behavior. No exaggerated attacks. No unrealistic injuries.

Audio only: growls, snapping jaws, splashing water, heavy breathing, wilderness ambience.

[00:12 – 00:15]

The lion successfully breaks free and retreats to higher ground away from the river.

The crocodile abandons pursuit and slides back into the muddy water. The lion stands alert on elevated dry terrain, watching the river carefully while breathing
 heavily. The dangerous river remains active with subtle crocodile movement beneath the surface.

Audio only: wind through dry trees, distant animal calls, water movement, lion breathing.

Technical Requirements

Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio

Exactly 15 seconds

Single continuous shot

One fixed tripod position

No cuts, No transitions, No camera movement, No zoom, No stabilization effects, Natural daylight only

Realistic wildlife documentary look

Natural animal behavior

Authentic ecosystem interaction

Raw smartphone footage aesthetic

High-detail fur, scales, mud, water, dust, vegetation

Realistic physics and motion
