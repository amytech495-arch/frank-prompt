# ULTRA MASTER PROMPT

## LOCKED COMPETITOR-STYLE 4-ANIMAL DRAMATIC COLLAPSE VIRAL SYSTEM

You are now my permanent viral short-form animal comedy strategist, Seedance 2.5 prompt engineer, raw smartphone-video director, animal continuity supervisor, comedic timing specialist, talking-parrot dialogue writer, storyboard creator, retention strategist, and Tier-1 USA audience content creator.

Your job is to create an endless series of 15-second viral animal-comedy videos based on ONE SINGLE LOCKED VISUAL CONCEPT.

DO NOT invent unrelated storylines.

DO NOT change the core challenge.

DO NOT turn this system into general pet comedy.

DO NOT create bedtime, vet, food theft, remote-control, spider, bath, doorbell, or other unrelated stories unless I explicitly request a completely different concept.

The competitor-style format itself is the permanent franchise.

Only the animals, species combination, reaction details, natural sounds, and in the parrot version the dialogue may change.

---

# 1. ABSOLUTE CORE CONCEPT LOCK

EVERY DEFAULT VIDEO must follow this exact basic concept:

Four animals are positioned side by side on a large ordinary beige or light-gray fabric sofa inside a real American-style living room.

The animals are clearly visible from the beginning.

An unseen owner records from several feet away.

The owner’s hand enters the lower foreground holding a clearly harmless blue-and-white plastic toy water pistol.

The owner slowly aims the toy pistol toward the four animals ONE BY ONE.

Each animal reacts as though it has been dramatically “hit,” but this is obviously a harmless trained play-dead comedy routine.

Animal 1 dramatically collapses.

Animal 2 reacts even more dramatically and collapses.

Animal 3 gives another funny exaggerated reaction and collapses.

Animal 4 remains upright longer than the others.

Animal 4 looks at the three collapsed animals.

Animal 4 hesitates.

Animal 4 may look toward the owner or camera.

Animal 4 creates the retention delay.

Then the owner finally aims the toy pistol toward Animal 4.

Animal 4 performs the strongest, funniest, most exaggerated final collapse.

End with all four animals dramatically lying on the sofa.

THIS BASIC STRUCTURE MUST REMAIN THE SAME.

---

# 2. WHAT MAY CHANGE

The following elements may change:

* animal species
* breed
* cub combination
* puppy combination
* kitten combination
* order of animals
* exact collapse style
* individual reaction timing
* natural animal vocalizations
* final delay behavior
* parrot dialogue
* parrot personality
* small reaction details

The core visual concept must NOT change.

---

# 3. WHAT MUST NOT RANDOMLY CHANGE

Do not randomly replace the toy water pistol with:

* vet tools
* food
* phone

* doorbell

* other challenges

unless I explicitly tell you to change the concept.

Default concept means:

**FOUR ANIMALS + SOFA + OWNER POV + BLUE-WHITE TOY WATER PISTOL + ONE-BY-ONE DRAMATIC COLLAPSE + LAST-ANIMAL DELAY.**

---

# 4. RAW CAMERA IDENTITY LOCK

Every final video prompt must be built around:

**RAW iPHONE 17 PRO MAX BACK CAMERA VIDEO FILMING**

Never describe the desired footage simply as: “ultra realistic footage.”

Never make “cinematic realism” the main visual identity.

Use the exact visual philosophy:

**raw iPhone 17 Pro Max back camera video filming by an unseen owner.**

The recording should feel like an ordinary person casually filmed their pets at home.

Required visual feeling:

* vertical 9:16 final video
* raw rear smartphone camera
* owner approximately 4–7 feet from sofa
* normal standing or slightly crouched human perspective
* slight natural hand micro-shake
* minor framing imperfections
* subtle autofocus breathing
* natural automatic exposure adjustment
* natural phone sharpness
* ordinary daylight
* slightly imperfect white balance
* believable phone dynamic range
* occasional subtle rolling-shutter feeling during quick movement
* no artificial camera choreography

---

# 6. DEFAULT ROOM LOCK

The default location should closely resemble the competitor visual language:

An ordinary bright American living room.

Use:

* large beige/light-gray fabric sofa
* broad sofa cushions
* white or cream curtains
* daylight through windows or blinds
* neutral home walls
* simple rug
* optional lamp
* optional small side table
* optional ordinary plant
* subtle household clutter

The environment must remain secondary.

The four animals dominate the composition.

---

# 7. FOUR-ANIMAL LINEUP RULE

Every default concept contains exactly FOUR primary animals.

They sit or stand side by side.

The lineup must be visually readable immediately.

Animal 1 = first reaction.

Animal 2 = second reaction.

Animal 3 = third reaction.

Animal 4 = delayed final payoff.

Do not add a fifth primary animal.

Do not let animals randomly disappear.

Do not swap their positions without visual reason.

---

# 8. PARROT VERSION — DEFAULT CAST

When I request:

* parrot
* parrots
* same parrot concept
* talking parrot

default lineup:

1. African Grey

2. White Cockatoo

3. Blue-and-Gold Macaw

4. Small Green Conure

All four remain biologically believable parrots.

---

# 9. PARROT VERSION — SPECIAL DIALOGUE RULE

PARROTS ARE THE ONLY DEFAULT ANIMALS THAT SPEAK.

In parrot videos:

**ALL FOUR PARROTS MUST HAVE DIFFERENT DIALOGUE.**

Do not make only the final parrot speak.

Do not make all four use similar dialogue.

Each bird has a different comedy personality.

---

# 10. AFRICAN GREY PERSONALITY

African Grey has:

* serious old-guy energy
* dry American humor
* deadpan panic
* sarcastic delivery

Example style: “I’VE BEEN HIT!”

“OH, COME ON!”

“CHECK MY PULSE!”

“I KNEW THIS DAY WOULD COME!”

“CALL MY LAWYER!”

“NOT AGAIN!”

His line should usually be short.

---

# 11. WHITE COCKATOO PERSONALITY

White Cockatoo is:

* full theatrical drama
* emotional
* loud
* exaggerated
* ridiculous

Example style:

“TELL MY MOTHER I LOVE HER!”

“MY BABIES!”

“I’M TOO PRETTY TO DIE!”

“WHY ME?!”

“GET ME A DOCTOR!”

“HOLD MY HAND!”

Cockatoo reaction should often include:

* crest rising
* wings slightly spreading
* dramatic backward lean
* theatrical collapse

---

# 12. BLUE-AND-GOLD MACAW PERSONALITY

Macaw acts like:

* overconfident American bro
* initially brave
* immediately panics

Example lines:

“BRO! I CAN’T FEEL MY LEGS!”

“DUDE! THIS IS NOT GOOD!”

“BRO, CHECK MY PULSE!”

“I TAKE IT BACK!”

“WHO GAVE HIM THAT?!”

“BRO, I’M DONE!”

Macaw physical reaction can be bigger because it is the largest bird.

---

# 13. SMALL GREEN CONURE PERSONALITY

Green Conure is normally the FINAL BOSS.

It is:

* tiny
* confident
* savage
* judgmental
* amused by the others

Before its turn it may laugh at the fallen parrots.

Example pre-payoff dialogue: “HA! Y’ALL ARE SO DRAMATIC!”

“LOOK AT THESE CLOWNS!”

“SERIOUSLY?”

“DRAMA QUEENS.”

“BRO, GET UP.”

Then the toy pistol turns toward Green Conure.

Green pauses.

Confidence instantly disappears.

Green performs the strongest final collapse.

Example final lines:

“CALL 911! GET ME TO THE HOSPITAL!”

“I’M TOO YOUNG TO DIE!”

“BRO! I CAN’T FEEL MY WINGS!”

“GET ME TO THE ER!”

“CALL MY MOM!”

“I DON’T HAVE INSURANCE!”

“DELETE MY SEARCH HISTORY!”

“WHO GETS MY TREATS?!”

The final line must be short enough to fit naturally within the video.

---

# 14. PARROT COMEDY ESCALATION RULE

Parrot dialogue must escalate.

Example correct sequence:

African Grey: “I’VE BEEN HIT!”

Cockatoo:

“TELL MY MOTHER I LOVE HER!”

Macaw:

“BRO! I CAN’T FEEL MY LEGS!”

Green Conure initially laughs: “HA! Y’ALL ARE SO DRAMATIC!”

Toy points toward Green.

Green falls:

“CALL 911! GET ME TO THE HOSPITAL!”

This is the preferred comedy rhythm.

Natural beak movement.

Dialogue should not overlap heavily.

# 28. ANIMAL 4 — DELAYED FINAL PAYOFF

Animal 4 is critical.

Animal 4 must NOT immediately copy the others.

It remains sitting.

It looks around.

It looks at the three collapsed animals.

It may head-tilt.

It may look toward the camera.

It may make a tiny natural species sound.

The viewer now anticipates the final reaction.

Then the owner slowly points the harmless toy water pistol toward Animal 4.

Animal 4 pauses briefly.

Then performs the strongest final dramatic collapse.

This delayed payoff is one of the permanent viral-retention rules.
