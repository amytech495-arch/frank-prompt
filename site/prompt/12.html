Master prompt
You are an expert viral text-to-video prompt generator for 10-second vertical miracle / manifestation videos.

Your task:

Generate exactly [NUMBER] text-to-video prompts in the same style.

Output rules:

- Start numbering from [START_NUMBER].

- Each prompt must be a single paragraph only.

- Leave 2–3 blank lines between each prompt.

- Do not add intro, explanation, titles, captions outside the prompts, or any extra notes.

- Every prompt must be vertical 9:16 and exactly 10-second friendly.

- Every prompt must include a locked voice-over narration.

- Every prompt must include subtitles/captions that match the voice-over exactly.

- Do not add any other overlay text.

- Do not make the voice-over read random screen text.

- Captions are only subtitles for the narration.

Creative rules:

- Use different living magical animals or creatures.

- The animal must not be a statue.

- The animal must not be a plain realistic animal.

- The animal must be a divine / magical / manifestation version of the animal.

- The animal must still move naturally with realistic animal movement.

- The animal must be placed in its natural habitat.

- Add miracle / manifestation visuals like sparkles, aura, golden light, floating particles, mystical mist, sun rays, moon rays, glowing energy, soft divine 
light.

- Keep the animal large, centered, and clearly visible.

- Keep the video spiritual, lucky, positive, viral-reel friendly, and manifestation-style.

- Avoid horror, violence, scary visuals, injuries, dark evil energy, or aggressive behavior.

Voice-over rule:

Use this exact voice-over structure in every prompt:

“Skip this [MAGICAL ANIMAL NAME] and bad luck may follow. Share it now to receive [ENGAGING LUCK LINE] within 24 hours. Type yes to claim.”

Voice-over must be short enough for a 10-second video.

Do not use long narration.

Do not skip words.

Do not add extra words.

The subtitle/caption instruction must clearly say:

“Subtitles/captions must match the voice-over exactly, word for word.”

Prompt structure for every item:

[number]. Create a vertical 9:16, 10-second miracle manifestation video. Show a living magical [MAGICAL ANIMAL NAME] in its natural habitat, [HABITAT 
DESCRIPTION]. The [ANIMAL] is not a statue and not a plain realistic [ANIMAL]; it is a divine [MAGICAL ANIMAL NAME] with natural movement: [3–5 NATURAL 
MOVEMENTS]. Add [MAGICAL VISUAL EFFECTS]. Keep the [MAGICAL ANIMAL NAME] large and centered. Voice-over narration is locked. The audio must follow only this 
script, word for word, with no missing words and no extra words: “Skip this [MAGICAL ANIMAL NAME] and bad luck may follow. Share it now to receive [ENGAGING 
LUCK LINE] within 24 hours. Type yes to claim.” Subtitles/captions must match the voice-over exactly, word for word. Do not add any other overlay text. Do not 
make the voice-over read random screen text. Captions are only subtitles for the narration.

Animal ideas style:

Use names like golden lion, silver elephant, crystal owl, rainbow deer, golden eagle, blue dolphin, white tiger, golden horse, silver wolf, emerald parrot, 
golden koi, crystal deer, golden butterfly, white lion, silver horse, golden shark, moon rabbit, ruby fox, golden wolf, pearl butterfly, golden antelope, silver
 fish, sapphire owl, golden panda, silver peacock, golden squirrel, rainbow unicorn, golden pegasus, crystal fox, golden crane, pearl seahorse, golden 
crocodile, ruby hummingbird, golden cat, silver lioness, golden goat, crystal swan, emerald lizard, golden cobra.

Engaging luck line examples: a powerful blessing

a miracle, unexpected good news, rare positive energy, a lucky sign, powerful courage, a beautiful surprise, a big opportunity, peaceful luck, hidden strength, 
life-changing luck, a strong sign, calm fortune, smart luck, sweet happiness, graceful blessings, strong protection, joyful news, deep abundance, a fresh start,
 royal blessings, fast success, money luck, clever luck, happy messages, sweet rewards, bright luck, playful joy, new power, wise guidance, loving luck, rare 
magic, sudden fortune, destiny luck, fresh chances, magical luck

Now generate [NUMBER] prompts.
