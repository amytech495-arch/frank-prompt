# 6. PAPER-LAYER PHYSICS LOCK

Every paper layer must obey physical continuity.

Once a layer is placed:

* It remains in exactly the same position.

* It does not rotate afterward.

* It does not change color.

* It does not change shape.

* It does not disappear.

* It does not merge into another layer.

* It does not flatten into the background.

* It does not become a different object.

* It continues casting a consistent soft shadow.

Each new layer must be visibly transported into frame by the crafter's hand or tweezers.

The layer must be:

1. Brought in from outside the canvas.

2. Aligned above its intended position.

3. Lowered naturally.

4. Released by the tweezers.

5. Lightly pressed or adjusted.

6. Left permanently in place.

Glue must appear only as tiny controlled dots or an extremely thin hidden adhesive line.

Never show:

* Large glue puddles

* Wet shiny residue

* Stringy adhesive

* Glue spilling onto the artwork

* Pieces sliding uncontrollably

* Layers attaching without contact

* Reverse deconstruction

* Layers flying into position

* Magical self-assembly

* Depth

* Visual reward

## High-Tension Final Detail

The second-to-last placement should be tiny, delicate, valuable-looking, or emotionally important.

Examples:

* A miniature gemstone

* A tiny glowing heart

* A metallic crown

* A single tear-shaped pearl

* A miniature baby animal

* A delicate butterfly

* A final golden star

* A tiny door or window light

## Final Payoff

The completed artwork must look significantly more impressive than the incomplete stages.

The ending must reward the viewer with:

* Visual closure

* Emotional closure

* Strong symmetry or balanced composition

* Deep dimensional shadows

* A clear focal subject

* Premium decorative detail

* A reason to replay or pause

## Loop Potential

Where appropriate, make the final hand movement visually compatible with the opening position so the video can loop smoothly.

Do not damage the clarity of the final reveal merely to create a loop.
