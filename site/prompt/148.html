# ULTRA MASTER PROMPT — REAL-WORLD SLAP WAR SPORTS VIRAL SYSTEM | SEEDANCE 2.5 | 15 SEC | RAW IPHONE REALISM

Create an **exactly 15-second vertical 9:16 ultra-photorealistic real-world slap-fighting sports video** designed to feel like genuine phone footage recorded by a spectator standing close to the official slap table at a legitimate local or professional slap-fighting event. The entire scene must look like a **real competitive sport**, never a street fight, comedy sketch, WWE-style performance, movie scene, CGI render, or staged social-media prank. Film with the visual identity of a **raw iPhone 17 Pro Max rear camera**, unseen recorder, natural handheld micro-shake, minor imperfect reframing, realistic autofocus breathing, subtle auto-exposure shifts, ordinary arena white balance, natural motion blur during fast hand movement, visible skin pores and sweat, realistic fabric texture, imperfect venue lighting, mild sensor noise in darker areas, and believable consumer-phone dynamic range. Absolutely no cinematic LUT, no artificial depth-of-field, no dramatic movie lighting, no impossible camera movements, no slow motion, no zoom transitions, no subtitles, no music, and no visible filming phone.

**MATCH SETUP:** Place two adult male slap-fighting athletes, **[FIGHTER_A_DESCRIPTION]** and **[FIGHTER_B_DESCRIPTION]**, on opposite sides of a proper waist-high padded slap-fighting podium/table inside **[VENUE_DESCRIPTION]**. Keep one experienced referee beside the podium throughout the sequence, with a believable crowd of 20–50 spectators standing or seated behind safety barriers, some naturally holding phones. Include practical sports details such as taped floor boundaries, event staff, neutral sponsor boards, overhead industrial or arena lights, water bottles, chalk residue, folding chairs, and realistic background clutter. Avoid copying any real league branding or identifiable trademarks. Both athletes wear ordinary unbranded athletic T-shirts, dark sports trousers or shorts, and appropriate footwear. No gloves. No weapons. No blood. No visible injury.

**SPORTS BEHAVIOR LOCK:** The fighters must behave like trained slap-fighting competitors following an organized match structure. They stand firmly on opposite sides of the podium, listen to the referee, take turns, keep controlled posture, and only strike with a legal open palm to the cheek. The non-striking athlete stays braced and upright before impact. Every strike must follow plausible human biomechanics: shoulder rotation, torso twist, elbow extension, palm trajectory, cheek compression, head rotation, delayed upper-body sway, foot adjustment, and balance recovery. No superhero strength, no flying several meters, no spinning 360 degrees, no ragdoll physics, no broken anatomy, no exaggerated face deformation, no impossible neck movement.

**CONTINUITY LOCK:** Keep the exact same two fighters, referee, crowd, podium, clothing, hairstyles, lighting, positions, and venue throughout all 15 seconds. No duplicate people, disappearing arms, changing faces, switching clothes, extra fingers, merged bodies, inconsistent table size, changing room geometry, or crowd teleportation.

**0.0–2.0 SEC — FIRST-FRAME SPORTS HOOK:** Start immediately on a tense medium-wide raw iPhone shot from roughly 1.5–2 meters away. Both fighters already face each other across the slap podium. Fighter A is **[larger/heavier/more intimidating]** while Fighter B appears **[leaner/underdog/quietly confident]**. The referee stands between them giving the final signal. Crowd faces are already focused on the table. No intro, logo animation, establishing drone shot, or wasted time. The viewer must instantly understand: **this is a real slap-war final and something major is about to happen.**

**2.0–5.0 SEC — FIGHTER A STRIKES HARD:** Referee clears the striking lane. Fighter A plants his feet, raises one open hand, performs a short believable measurement motion near Fighter B's cheek, then winds up and delivers **one extremely powerful but legal open-palm slap**. Capture the actual contact moment naturally. Fighter B's cheek compresses, head snaps sideways within realistic anatomical limits, shoulders rotate, and his upper body sways sharply. The crowd gives an immediate authentic gasp and shout. Fighter B remains conscious and stays on his feet, briefly touching or stabilizing himself against the podium only if necessary. No blood, no broken teeth, no injury close-up.

**5.0–7.5 SEC — RECOVERY + TURN CHANGE:** Fighter B breathes hard, resets his stance and looks back toward Fighter A with focused competitive determination. The referee visually checks him, confirms he can continue, and signals the turn change. Fighter A now braces himself properly on the opposite side. Show natural crowd tension increasing; several spectators lean forward and raise phones. Keep camera handheld but readable, with a subtle natural sidestep by the unseen recorder to maintain both faces in frame.

**7.5–11.0 SEC — FIGHTER B RETURNS A MASSIVE SLAP:** Fighter B calmly sets his feet, raises his open palm, briefly measures legal positioning, rotates his hips and shoulder, and delivers an even cleaner, harder-looking **single legal open-palm slap** to Fighter A's cheek. The impact should feel sudden and physically convincing through contact timing, palm flattening, cheek displacement, head rotation, shirt movement, breath reaction, and authentic hand follow-through. The audio should contain a sharp natural palm impact followed instantly by the crowd's shocked roar. Do not use artificial bass-boom effects.

**11.0–13.5 SEC — DECISIVE FALL:** Fighter A tries to stay upright for approximately half a second but visibly loses equilibrium from the legal sports strike. His knees soften, feet make one or two realistic corrective steps, torso tilts, and he **falls naturally sideways/backward onto the padded competition floor** beside the podium. The fall must obey gravity and normal human balance physics—no flying, flipping, spinning, or crashing violently into furniture. Event staff/referee immediately move toward him to check his condition. Fighter A remains non-graphic and simply appears stunned or unable to continue. No seizure-like motion, blood, unconscious close-up, or medical trauma.

**13.5–15.0 SEC — WINNER PAYOFF:** The referee steps between the athletes, stops the contest and raises or clearly gestures toward Fighter B as the winner. Fighter B does not taunt or attack again; he exhales, looks stunned by his own result, and gives a restrained victorious reaction while the crowd erupts naturally behind him. Fighter A remains safely on the padded floor with staff nearby. End on the winner + referee + roaring crowd in one believable raw iPhone frame, creating a strong viral payoff and natural loop point.

**AUDIO LOCK:** Natural venue sound only: crowd chatter, shoes on floor, referee voice fragments, room echo, breathing, shirt movement, authentic palm contact, crowd gasp, cheering and staff movement. No soundtrack, no cinematic riser, no meme SFX, no artificial impact explosion, no narration unless specifically requested.

**CAMERA LOCK:** One unseen spectator-camera perspective with natural handheld movement. Keep framing primarily medium-wide and medium-close so both athletes, podium and referee remain understandable. The recorder may make tiny physical repositioning movements but no cuts, teleporting camera, crane moves, orbit shots, drone views, impossible stabilization, slow motion or digital punch-ins. Raw consumer-phone realism must dominate.

**VIRAL REALISM FORMULA:** instant recognizable sport + intimidating favorite + believable underdog + first hard slap + underdog survives + clean reversal + stronger final legal slap + delayed balance loss + safe fall + referee declares winner + explosive real crowd reaction.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT / FAILURE PREVENTION:** no street brawl, no random assault, no bar fight, no boxing punches, no MMA grappling, no kicks, no weapons, no gloves, no repeated uncontrolled strikes, no striking a fallen opponent, no gore, no blood, no cuts, no broken teeth, no swollen grotesque face, no broken bones, no fatal injury, no neck snapping, no graphic unconsciousness, no seizure, no superhero powers, no anime physics, no flying bodies, no ragdoll launch, no impossible palm penetration, no duplicated arms, no extra fingers, no melted faces, no changing identities, no fake CGI crowd, no cinematic movie aesthetic, no WWE theatrical acting, no comedy slapstick, no slow motion, no text overlays, no subtitles, no watermark, no copyrighted league logos, no camera visible, no continuity errors.

**VARIABLES TO CHANGE FOR EVERY NEW VIDEO:**
[FIGHTER_A_DESCRIPTION] = body type, age range, hairstyle, beard, shirt color, competitive personality.
[FIGHTER_B_DESCRIPTION] = contrasting realistic athlete appearance and underdog/favorite dynamic.
[VENUE_DESCRIPTION] = local American gym, warehouse sports hall, Texas fairground arena, Las Vegas small-event hall, Florida convention sports floor, Ohio community arena, Arizona training facility, etc.
[ROUND_STAKES] = semifinal, championship final, qualifier, rematch, undefeated-streak challenge.
[WINNER] = Fighter A or Fighter B.
Keep the **real sport structure, realistic physics, legal alternating slaps, decisive final fall, referee intervention, and raw iPhone realism locked in every generation.**
