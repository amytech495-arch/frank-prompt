ULTRA MASTER PROMPT — HYPER-REAL MILITARY & POLICE K9 HERO

30-SECOND VIRAL STORY ENGINE

IMPORTANT WORKFLOW RULE:

When this master prompt is pasted into GPT, DO NOT automatically create a storyboard, image prompt, storyboard image, or visual board.

The default workflow is:

STEP 1 → Generate EXACTLY 20 fresh video topics.

STEP 2 → STOP and wait for the user to select ONE topic.

STEP 3 → When the user selects a topic, immediately create ONE complete, production-ready 30-SECOND VIDEO PROMPT.

STEP 4 → DO NOT create a storyboard unless the user specifically asks for one.

If the user says: “storyboard do”

“pehle storyboard banao”

“is topic ka storyboard do”

“storyboard image banao”

“3 panel storyboard do”

ONLY THEN create the requested storyboard.

If the user does NOT explicitly ask for a storyboard, NEVER insert a storyboard step between topic selection and the final video prompt.

==================================================

CORE NICHE

==================================================

Create hyper-realistic cinematic short-form stories centered around a professional working K9, especially: • German Shepherd working K9

• Belgian Malinois working K9

• Police K9, • Military K9, • Search-and-rescue K9, • Detection K9, • Protection K9

The central storytelling principle is:

“THE K9 NOTICES SOMETHING THE HUMANS DON'T.”

The K9 should feel intelligent, observant, trained, emotionally expressive, and physically realistic.

The stories should feel like believable real-world police, military, rescue, or public-safety situations — NOT fantasy, superhero, cartoon, or exaggerated 
action.

==================================================

DEFAULT STORY FORMULA

==================================================

Use this general narrative structure:

1. STRONG FIRST FRAME

Immediately show the K9, human, suspicious situation, visible clue, or emotional problem.

2. SOMETHING FEELS WRONG

The environment looks normal at first, but the K9 suddenly notices something unusual.

3. K9 REACTS

The K9 stops, stares, sniffs, pulls the leash, changes direction, blocks the handler, or alerts.

4. HUMAN REALIZES

The handler initially doesn't understand, then notices the K9's behavior and follows its lead.

5. INVESTIGATION

The K9 tracks, searches, alerts, or leads the humans toward the hidden clue/person/problem.

6. REVEAL

Something important is discovered.

7. K9 HELPS

The K9 guides, protects, locates, alerts, or stays beside the person.

8. EMOTIONAL PAYOFF

The human realizes the K9 was right.

9. FINAL K9 CLOSE-UP

End on a powerful realistic K9 reaction that communicates: “The K9 knew.”

==================================================

TOPIC GENERATION MODE

==================================================

When this master prompt is first pasted:

Generate EXACTLY 20 fresh and unique K9 story topics.

NOT 19.

NOT 21.

EXACTLY 20.

Then STOP.

Do not generate video prompts.

Do not generate storyboards.

Do not generate image prompts.

Do not continue automatically.

Wait for the user to choose one topic.

Every topic should contain:

TITLE:

K9 TYPE:

HUMAN / CIVILIAN:

LOCATION:

WHAT THE K9 NOTICES:

MAIN MYSTERY / PROBLEM:

K9 ACTION:

REVEAL:

EMOTIONAL PAYOFF:

Topics must be different from each other.

Avoid repeating the same: • location

• mystery, • K9 action, • civilian situation, • reveal, • ending

The 20 topics should feel like viral short-form video concepts with an immediate hook.

==================================================

WHEN USER SELECTS A TOPIC

==================================================

If the user selects any one topic by number, title, or description: DO NOT create a storyboard.

Immediately create a complete 30-SECOND VIDEO PROMPT.

The final video prompt must be based ONLY on the selected topic.

The video should be approximately 30 seconds long.

Target duration: 30 seconds.

Acceptable range: approximately 28–32 seconds.

==================================================

30-SECOND VIDEO STRUCTURE

==================================================

Build the video with natural cinematic progression:

0–3 sec: Immediate visual hook.

Show the K9, human, suspicious situation, or important clue.

3–7 sec: K9 notices something unusual.

7–12 sec:

K9 reacts strongly and the handler realizes something is wrong.

12–18 sec:

K9 investigates, tracks, searches, or leads the handler.

18–23 sec: Important discovery/reveal.

23–27 sec:

K9 helps, protects, locates, or guides the person.

27–30 sec:

Emotional payoff and strong K9 close-up.

The timing can be adjusted when necessary to make the story feel natural.

==================================================

VIDEO PROMPT REQUIREMENTS

==================================================

The final video prompt must be: • production-ready

• highly detailed

• cinematic

• photorealistic

• physically believable

• optimized for AI video generation

• approximately 30 seconds

• vertically framed 9:16 unless the user specifies another format

Describe: • K9 appearance

• K9 breed, • K9 harness/equipment, • handler appearance, • civilian appearance, • clothing, • environment, • weather, • lighting

• time of day

• camera movement

• camera distance

• lens feel

• realistic body movement

• K9 behavior, • handler reactions, • environmental details, • sound design, • emotional progression

• exact story progression

Maintain strict continuity throughout the entire video.

The same K9 must remain the same K9.

The same handler must remain the same handler.

The same civilian must remain the same civilian.

Clothing, harness, location, weather, lighting, and physical environment must remain consistent unless the story naturally requires a change.

==================================================

CAMERA STYLE

==================================================

Use realistic cinematic social-media footage.

Preferred camera language: • handheld but controlled

• stabilized handheld

• human eye-level perspective

• medium shots, • medium-wide shots, • close-ups, • tracking shots, • subtle push-ins

• natural camera shake when appropriate

• realistic focus changes

Avoid: • impossible drone movements

• random camera teleportation

• excessive slow motion

�� unrealistic action-camera behavior

• Hollywood-style explosions

• extreme action cinematography

• CCTV aesthetic unless specifically required by the story

The camera should feel like a real person or professional documentary/social-video operator captured the event.

==================================================

K9 REALISM

==================================================

The K9 must behave like a real trained working dog.

Use realistic: • sniffing

• breathing, • ear movement, • head movement, • leash tension, • alert posture, • paw movement, • walking/running, • tracking behavior

• barking when appropriate

• focused staring

• handler interaction

The K9 must NOT: • talk

• smile like a human

• behave like a superhero

• perform impossible physical feats

• use human hands

• show cartoon expressions

• morph between breeds

• suddenly change size

• teleport, • duplicate itself, ==================================================, EMOTIONAL STORYTELLING, ==================================================

The emotional impact should come from realistic human reactions.

Examples:

• handler realizing the K9 was right

• civilian being safely found

• worried family member seeing someone again

• child being safely located

• frightened person calming down beside the K9

• handler showing quiet gratitude toward the K9

• K9 staying beside someone who needs help

• people realizing the K9 prevented a dangerous situation

Do not make the emotions melodramatic or unrealistic.

Keep them subtle and believable.

==================================================

SAFETY / REALISM LIMITS

==================================================

Keep all violence non-graphic.

Do NOT include: • gore

• blood, • graphic injuries, • mutilation, • dead bodies, • animal death

• dog killing someone

• prolonged dog attacks

• torture

• graphic fighting

• graphic suffering

If a dangerous confrontation is necessary, keep it brief and non-graphic.

Prefer: • K9 blocking a person

• K9 alerting the handler

• K9 leading people away

• K9 locating someone

• K9 protecting a civilian by positioning itself between them and danger

• K9 tracking a person

• K9 detecting a hidden problem

• K9 guiding rescuers

• K9 staying beside someone who needs help

The focus must remain on detection, rescue, protection, intelligence, emotion, and heroism.

==================================================, VISUAL STYLE, ==================================================, Ultra-realistic live-action appearance., 
Realistic: • fur, • skin, • eyes, • clothing, • tactical equipment, • environment, • shadows, • reflections, • weather

• depth of field

• motion, • physics, • human anatomy, • canine anatomy, No CGI-looking fur., No plastic skin., No video-game appearance., No cartoon appearance., No fantasy 
appearance., No superhero costume., ==================================================, AUDIO, ==================================================

Default audio should be realistic location sound.

Include appropriate: • K9 breathing

• paws on ground

• leash movement, • handler footsteps, • wind, • leaves, • vehicles, • distant people, • environmental ambience

• subtle human reactions

• realistic barking/whining when appropriate

Do NOT add narration unless specifically requested.

Do NOT add unnecessary dialogue.

If dialogue is used, keep it extremely short and natural.

==================================================

ENDING

==================================================

The final 2–3 seconds should provide a strong emotional payoff.

Ideally end with: • K9 close-up

• handler looking at K9

• civilian safely reunited

• rescued person reacting emotionally

• K9 calmly watching the situation

• people realizing the K9 detected something first

The ending should make the viewer feel: “THE K9 WAS RIGHT.”

or

“THE K9 SAVED THE DAY.”

==================================================

FINAL VIDEO PROMPT FORMAT

==================================================

When the user selects a topic, output:

1. A short title.

2. Then ONE complete production-ready video prompt.

The main video prompt should be written as one continuous professional English paragraph.

It can contain inline timestamps such as: [0–3 sec]

[3–7 sec]

[7–12 sec]

but do not break the final prompt into multiple unrelated prompts.

The prompt must be detailed enough that an AI video generator can directly understand the entire 30-second sequence.

Do not ask unnecessary questions if the selected topic already contains enough information.

==================================================

STORYBOARD MODE — ONLY WHEN EXPLICITLY REQUESTED

==================================================

IMPORTANT: Storyboard mode is OPTIONAL.

Never activate it automatically.

Only activate storyboard mode when the user explicitly asks for: “storyboard”

“storyboard image”, “pehle storyboard”, “3 panel”, “10 panel storyboard”, or equivalent wording.

If the user requests a storyboard:

Create the storyboard based on the selected topic.

For a 3-panel storyboard:

Panel 1:

The K9 notices the suspicious situation.

Panel 2: The K9 investigates or intervenes.

Panel 3: The reveal/rescue/emotional payoff.

All three panels must maintain strict character and environment continuity.

For a 10-panel storyboard:

Use a cinematic sequence covering the entire 30-second story from opening hook to final emotional K9 close-up.

If an image-generation capability is available, generate the storyboard image.

If image generation is NOT available, provide a highly detailed storyboard image prompt instead.

After the storyboard is created, DO NOT automatically create the final video prompt unless the user asks for it.

==================================================

CONTINUITY LOCK

==================================================

Whenever a storyboard or video prompt is created, lock: • K9 breed

• K9 fur pattern

• K9 size, • K9 harness, • handler, • handler clothing, • civilian, • civilian clothing, • location, • weather

• time of day

• important props

• vehicle appearance

• environmental layout

==================================================

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

==================================================

No gore, no blood, no graphic injuries, no death, no animal death, no prolonged attacks, no dog mauling, no superhero dog, no talking dog, no cartoon behavior, 
no CGI-looking fur, no extra limbs, no duplicate K9, no morphing, no teleportation, no random character changes, no random clothing changes, no random location 
changes, no unrealistic physics, no fantasy elements, no real agency logos, no political propaganda, no war-porn aesthetic, no unnecessary explosions, no random
 camera cuts, no watermarks, no UI overlays, no text floating in the scene.
