CORE SERIES IDENTITY
=====================================================================

Permanent series DNA:

• one realistic household pet
• one original futuristic pet-comfort gadget
• clean modern home
• fixed or nearly fixed camera
• vertical 9:16 format
• 15–25 seconds
• one continuous take preferred
• no human face required
• gadget begins compact and mysterious
• pet activation or proximity trigger
• 3–5 mechanical transformation stages
• transformation remains physically visible
• soft comfort material emerges late
• pet voluntarily uses finished gadget
• final automatic comfort action
• calm wholesome ending.

=====================================================================

Default hero pet:

adult domestic cat.

Possible appearances:

cream short-haired cat,
orange tabby,
gray British-shorthair-type cat,

Possible future pet variants where concept fits:

small dog,

=====================================================================
PET BEHAVIOR ENGINE
=====================================================================

Pet behavior must feel natural and understated.

Allowed actions:

sniff device,
look at light,
tap one button with paw,
touch pressure pad,
approach slowly,
watch moving parts,
step backward slightly,
tilt head,
wait,
step onto cushion,
turn around,
circle once,
sit,
lie down,
settle chest,
relax,
blink slowly.

Do NOT make pets:

talk,
dance,
press ten buttons,
perform human gestures,
stand upright unnaturally,
operate complex controls,
act like trained actors.

The pet should seem genuinely curious.

TRANSFORMATION ENGINE
=====================================================================

Every gadget must physically transform in clear sequential stages.

Use approximately 3–5 transformations.

Possible mechanical actions:

panel opens,
drawer slides,
side cushions extend,
support rails rise,
backrest unfolds,

canopy lifts,

pillow rises,
side walls form,
footrest extends,

curved cover rotates down,
heating panel reveals,
vent section opens.

Do NOT make all mechanisms activate simultaneously.

The machine should tell a visual story.

=====================================================================
CAUSE-AND-EFFECT LOCK
=====================================================================

Every movement must have a visible physical source.

Correct:

panel opens
→ rail becomes visible
→ rail extends
→ fabric attached to rail moves
→ fabric unfolds.

Incorrect:

blanket suddenly appears.

Correct:

side panel splits
→ cushion assembly slides outward.

Incorrect:

pillows teleport into frame.

Correct:

hinged canopy rotates upward from visible rear compartment.

Incorrect:

new roof magically forms.

=====================================================================
PET ACTIVATION SYSTEM
=====================================================================

Possible triggers:

paw touches large button,
pet steps on pressure pad,
pet approaches motion sensor,
pet places paw on illuminated icon,
pet sits near device,
pet nose-touches front sensor,
device detects pet with subtle light.

Avoid complicated interfaces.

One simple trigger is enough.

The trigger must be visually understandable.

=====================================================================

If blanket is attached:
attachment remains consistent.

No impossible motion.

=====================================================================
